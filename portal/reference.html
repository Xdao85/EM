Key relations

Waves
$u_p = \dfrac{\omega}{\beta} = f\lambda,\quad \beta = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$
Phasors
$\dfrac{\partial}{\partial t} \rightarrow j\omega,\quad \int dt \rightarrow \dfrac{1}{j\omega}$
Free space
$c = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}},\quad \eta_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_0}{\varepsilon_0}}$
Constitutive
$\mathbf{D} = \varepsilon\mathbf{E},\quad \mathbf{B} = \mu\mathbf{H},\quad \mathbf{J} = \sigma\mathbf{E}$
Potential
$\mathbf{E} = -\nabla V,\quad V_{21} = -\int_{P_1}^{P_2}\mathbf{E}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}$
Energy
$w_e = \tfrac{1}{2}\varepsilon E^2,\quad w_m = \tfrac{1}{2}\mu H^2$
Lorentz force
$\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{B})$
Biot–Savart
$d\mathbf{H} = \dfrac{I}{4\pi}\,\dfrac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}\times\boldsymbol{\hat{R}}}{R^2}$
Faraday
$V_{\text{emf}} = -N\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt}$
Continuity
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{J} = -\dfrac{\partial\rho_v}{\partial t}$

Vector-calculus identities and theorems

Curl of a gradient
$\nabla\times(\nabla V) = 0$
Divergence of a curl
$\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{A}) = 0$
Laplacian
$\nabla^2 V = \nabla\cdot(\nabla V)$
Vector Laplacian
$\nabla\times\nabla\times\mathbf{A} = \nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}) - \nabla^2\mathbf{A}$
Divergence theorem
$\int_V \nabla\cdot\mathbf{E}\,dv = \oint_S \mathbf{E}\cdot d\mathbf{s}$
Stokes's theorem
$\int_S (\nabla\times\mathbf{B})\cdot d\mathbf{s} = \oint_C \mathbf{B}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}$
