Divergence & Stokes's Theorems

Vector Calculus · Integral Theorems

From Local Derivatives to Global Integrals

Divergence and curl are defined as limits of flux per volume and circulation per area. Adding those tiny contributions over a finite region, the interior pieces cancel and only the boundary survives. These two theorems turn Maxwell's equations back and forth between their differential and integral forms. The divergence theorem connects a closed surface to its enclosed volume; Stokes' theorem connects a loop to an open surface spanning it. Choose the theorem by the geometry of the integral, then fix its orientation.

Divergence Theorem
$$\int_V \nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}\,dv = \oint_S \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{s}$$

$S$ is the closed surface enclosing $V$, with $d\mathbf{s}$ pointing outward. Applied to $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{D} = \rho_{\mathrm{v}}$ it gives Gauss's law $\oint_S\mathbf{D}\cdot d\mathbf{s} = Q$.

Stokes's Theorem
$$\int_S (\nabla\times\mathbf{F})\cdot d\mathbf{s} = \oint_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$$

$C$ is the boundary of the open surface $S$; the direction of $C$ and of $d\mathbf{s}$ follow the right-hand rule. Applied to $\nabla\times\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J}$ it gives Ampère's law $\oint_C\mathbf{H}\cdot d\mathbf{l} = I$.

The volume integral uses $dv = R^2\sin\theta\,dR\,d\theta\,d\phi$ (sphere), $r\,dr\,d\phi\,dz$ (cylinder) or $dx\,dy\,dz$ (cube); the surface integral uses the matching $d\mathbf{s}$. Both converge to the same number as $N$ grows.

Interactive Simulation 2

$\int_S (\nabla\times\mathbf{F})\cdot d\mathbf{s} = \oint_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$

Three different surfaces share the same boundary $C$: the circle of radius $a$ centered at $(x_0, y_0)$ in the plane $z = z_0$, traversed counter-clockwise (seen from $+z$). With upward normals the curl flux is the same through all of them and equals the circulation around $C$. Changing the surface does not change the boundary integral when the field is smooth over the surfaces and the region between them. Reverse the contour and the compatible normal together to reverse both signs.
